Safari's cookies for EWeb

Choose the sites whose logins EWeb on your Atari is to have. Everything happens in this page, on this Mac: nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Open Safari's cookie file

Click the button. In the window that opens, press ⇧⌘G, paste this and press Return:

~/Library/Containers/com.apple.Safari/Data/Library/Cookies/Cookies.binarycookies

If the Mac says you may not open it, quit Safari first, or allow it once in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

2. Tick the sites

SiteCookiesLast used

login marks a site with cookies only a browser sends and only over a secure connection: usually a login.

3. Save the file for the Atari

Ticked: none

Copy safari-cookies.txt to the Atari and open it with EWeb's File > Import cookies…; let EWeb delete it afterwards. The file holds your logins in plain text until then. Some sites tie a login to the computer it was made on and will ask you to log in again; cookies Safari keeps only until it quits aren't in its file.
